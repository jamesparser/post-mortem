POST-Mortem

The house

Something walks on its fingers here. Put on your Quest 3, stand somewhere with room to turn around, and let him in.

  • He freezes when you look at him. He moves when you do not.
  • Hide and seek and the burial live in the menu. Press the brass button on your left wrist, or B on the controller.
  • Map your room first: Settings, Physical space, Space setup. Without a map he cannot see your walls. If the floor looks wrong, choose Set the floor in the menu.
  • Press Allow the microphone before you enter, so the spirit board can hear your questions.
  • Headphones are not needed. The sound is already beside you.

Back to the gate

Watch him. Then look away.
